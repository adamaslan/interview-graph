# NuWrrrld Financial (NWF): pitches at four lengths

Say each one out loud. Each pitch stands alone, and each longer one contains the shorter one.

## 10 seconds

NuWrrrld Financial is AI market research you can question. Its AI council, with one seat arguing against the case, gives calls a direction and confidence.

## 30 seconds

NuWrrrld Financial is an AI research app for markets. A council of specialist AI seats examines a ticker, and one seat exists only to argue against the case. Each call comes back with a direction, a confidence level and, when set, an invalidation level: the point where the thesis breaks. Recorded calls can be published on public verdict pages, and paper trading lets you test ideas. I built it end to end.

## 1 minute

Most market tools hand you a label: buy, sell, hold. NuWrrrld Financial is built to show its reasoning.

The core is an AI council of specialist seats: a short-term trader, a long-term investor, a risk seat that plays devil's advocate, macro and quant seats, and a chair who synthesizes. The trader seats' answers are parsed into a fixed format, direction, reason, invalidation level, and validated.

Around it sits a research workflow: a market overview, explained signals, Nu AI questions on any ticker, portfolio health, and paper trading. Recorded calls can be published on public verdict pages, so a call can be checked against what happened next.

It serves a newcomer who wants plain English and an active trader who wants evidence.

Next: simulated portfolios managed by AI, an arena to compare them, and monthly calls reviewed as recorded outcomes.

## 2 minutes

Market tools are good at confident answers and bad at showing their work. You get a label, you cannot question it, and nobody checks it afterward. NuWrrrld Financial is built the other way around.

It follows four steps: understand, investigate, compare, check.

Understand. A market overview and daily briefing give context before you pick a stock. A newcomer gets plain English. A trader gets the indicators behind each signal.

Investigate. This is the heart of it. I designed an AI council of six seats: a short-term trader, a long-term investor, a risk seat that plays devil's advocate, macro and quant seats, and a chair who writes the verdict. The trader seats' answers are parsed into a strict format, direction, reason, invalidation level, then validated, with a repair step when a model drifts. I care most about that risk seat. A call that has met its strongest objection is worth more than one that never did. You can also ask Nu AI about any ticker, with your watchlist and portfolio as context.

Compare. Review your watchlist, sectors and portfolio health. Paper trading tests an idea with no money at risk.

Check. Recorded calls carry direction, confidence, horizon and, when set, an invalidation level. They can be published on public verdict pages, so anyone can compare what was said with what happened.

Underneath, a signals engine scans a large universe of tickers on a schedule.

Where it goes next: AI-managed paper portfolios, an arena to compare their holdings, and frozen monthly stock calls reviewed as recorded outcomes. That turns calls into a track record, and a track record into an API other tools can use.

NuWrrrld Financial: market research you can question, and calls you can check.
